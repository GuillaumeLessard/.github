# Getting help with QECTOR

- **Questions, bugs and failed reproductions:** open an issue in the repository concerned, for example [qector-decoder](https://github.com/GuillaumeLessard/qector-decoder/issues) or [qector-claude-plugin](https://github.com/GuillaumeLessard/qector-claude-plugin/issues). Include the version, your platform and the smallest example that shows the problem.
- **Security problems:** do not open a public issue; see the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
- **Commercial licensing:** plans and terms are at [qector.store/pricing](https://qector.store/pricing).
- **Research records:** papers, proofs and datasets are in the [QECTOR Research Archive](https://zenodo.org/communities/id01t) on Zenodo. Each record says how to cite it.
